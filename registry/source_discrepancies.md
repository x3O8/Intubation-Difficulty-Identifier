# Source discrepancies and inactive interpretations

The registry preserves, rather than resolves, the following conflicts. A qualified protocol owner must approve a precise interpretation before any corresponding clinical rule can become active.

- Modified Mallampati wording differs between slides 9 and 10. Slide 9 also mentions class 0; it is not silently inserted into the I–IV ordering. The app therefore permits only a human-reviewed grade under the complete posture/maneuver/visibility protocol.
- Slide 11's atlanto-occipital description uses an incisor/occlusal-plane concept and includes both 80–85° and 25–30° wording. The paper instead specifies a supine tragus-to-mouth-corner line relative to horizontal. They remain separate inactive protocols.
- The presentation's `>5 cm` mouth-opening normal reference, `<3 cm` difficult-laryngoscopy reference, and `<2 cm` supraglottic-device reference have different meanings and outcomes. None is activated without reviewed incisal endpoints, matching protocol, and valid centimetre calibration.
- Thyromental, sternomental, and related endpoint/posture descriptions differ across slides and paper. Generic external video and face mesh do not satisfy these protocols.
- Slide 16's illustrated grading labels must not override slide 15 or a formal Cormack–Lehane definition. External video cannot supply Cormack–Lehane or POGO evidence in any case.

The supplied paper's clinical measurements and model performance are reference material only. They do not validate transfer to this video collection, automatic measurement, participant identity, or overall intubation prediction.
